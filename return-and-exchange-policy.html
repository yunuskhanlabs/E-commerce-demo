LEGAL & POLICIES

Exchange & Refund Policy

Last Updated: 16 August 2026

1. Exchange Window

E-Commerce Demo offers a 7-day exchange window for eligible products.

Customers must raise an exchange request within 7 calendar days from the date of delivery.

Requests raised after this period may not be accepted.

The exchange window applies from the date the order is marked as delivered by the applicable delivery partner.

2. Exchange Eligibility

A product is eligible for exchange only if all of the following conditions are met:

  • The product is unused and unworn.
  • The product has not been washed, dry-cleaned, ironed, altered, or damaged.
  • All original tags and labels are attached and intact.
  • The product is in its original condition.
  • The original packaging is retained wherever applicable.
  • The product is returned in a condition suitable for resale.

E-Commerce Demo reserves the right to reject an exchange if the returned product does not meet these conditions.

3. Size Exchange

Size-related exchanges are available only for the same product, subject to size availability.

Customers may request a different size within the 7-day exchange window.

If the requested size is available, E-Commerce Demo will process the exchange.

If the requested replacement size is unavailable and there is no confirmed restock, E-Commerce Demo will process a refund for the eligible order.

Size-related exchanges are not eligible for a refund where a suitable replacement is available.

Customers are advised to check the Size Guide before placing an order.

4. One Exchange Per Order

Unless the product received is incorrect, damaged, or defective, only one exchange request per product/order will be permitted.

Once an exchanged product has been delivered, it will generally not be eligible for another size exchange.

5. Sale & Discounted Products

Products purchased during a sale or promotional offer are eligible for exchange subject to the same product-condition requirements stated above.

Size exchanges on sale or discounted products are subject to availability.

Any specific product marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Exchangeable" at the time of purchase will not be eligible for exchange, provided this is clearly disclosed before purchase.

6. Wrong, Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive:

  • A wrong product
  • A wrong size due to a fulfilment error
  • A damaged product
  • A defective product
  • A product materially different from the product description

please contact E-Commerce Demo as soon as possible after delivery.

Customers may be required to provide clear photographs or videos of the product, packaging, tags, and shipping label for verification.

After reviewing the issue, E-Commerce Demo will provide an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement, exchange, return, or refund, as applicable.

7. Exchange Request Process

To request an exchange, contact E-Commerce Demo within 7 calendar days of delivery.

Please provide:

  • Order number
  • Registered customer name and contact details
  • Product name
  • Reason for exchange
  • Requested replacement size, where applicable
  • Photographs or videos, if requested

Our team will review the request and confirm whether the product qualifies for exchange.

Customers should not ship the product back independently unless instructed by E-Commerce Demo.

8. Exchange Pickup & Shipping

For an approved exchange, E-Commerce Demo will arrange the applicable return pickup.

For eligible exchanges caused by a fulfilment error, wrong product, damage, or manufacturing defect, E-Commerce Demo will bear the applicable return shipping cost.

For a customer-requested size exchange, the applicable exchange shipping charges, if any, will be communicated to the customer before the exchange is processed.

The product must be securely packed and ready for pickup.

9. Inspection & Approval

All returned products are subject to inspection.

An exchange will be processed only after the returned product is received and verified to meet the applicable exchange conditions.

If the returned product fails inspection because it has been used, washed, damaged, altered, or returned without required tags, E-Commerce Demo may reject the exchange.

10. Refunds

E-Commerce Demo does not offer refunds for ordinary size or preference-based exchanges where an eligible replacement is available.

However, where an eligible wrong, damaged, defective, or materially misdescribed product cannot reasonably be replaced or exchanged, E-Commerce Demo may provide a refund or other appropriate resolution, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the case.

Where a refund is approved, it will be processed through the applicable payment method or refund process.

For Cash on Delivery orders, customers may be required to provide appropriate bank account or payment details for the refund.

11. Non-Eligible Exchanges

An exchange request may be rejected if:

  • The request is made after the 7-day exchange window.
  • The product has been worn or used.
  • The product has been washed or dry-cleaned.
  • The product has been altered, repaired, or damaged after delivery.
  • Original tags or labels are missing or damaged.
  • The product is returned without required packaging where applicable.
  • The product fails the quality inspection.
  • The product is marked as Final Sale or Non-Exchangeable.
  • The requested exchange does not meet the applicable product or order conditions.

12. Product Availability

All exchanges are subject to product and size availability.

If the requested replacement is unavailable, E-Commerce Demo may offer an alternative available option or another appropriate resolution.

13. Important Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit any rights or remedies available to customers under applicable consumer protection laws.

Where applicable law requires a return, replacement, refund, or other remedy for a defective, incorrect, misdescribed, or otherwise non-conforming product, E-Commerce Demo will comply with the applicable legal requirements.
