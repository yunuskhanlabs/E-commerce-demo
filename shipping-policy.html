LEGAL & POLICIES

Shipping Policy

Last Updated: 16 August 2026

1. Shipping Availability

We aim to provide delivery services across India.

Shipping availability may vary depending on the delivery location, serviceable pincode, and courier partner availability.

2. Order Processing

Orders are processed after successful order confirmation.

Processing and dispatch timelines may vary depending on product availability, order volume, and other operational factors.

Once your order has been dispatched, you may receive shipping and tracking information where available.

3. Delivery Timeline

Delivery timelines vary depending on the customer's location, serviceable pincode, courier network, and other shipping conditions.

The estimated delivery timeline, where available, may be provided during the order process or through the order tracking information.

Delivery timelines are estimates and are not guaranteed. Delays may occur due to circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated based on delivery location, package weight, dimensions, and courier partner availability.

Applicable shipping charges will be displayed to the customer during checkout before the order is placed.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, tracking information may be provided through the contact details associated with your order.

You can use the provided tracking information to check the current status of your shipment.

6. Delivery Attempts

Our courier partners may make delivery attempts according to their delivery procedures.

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and being available to receive the order when required.

If a delivery cannot be completed because of an incorrect or incomplete address, unavailability of the recipient, or other customer-related reasons, additional delivery attempts or charges may apply where applicable.

7. Delayed Deliveries

Delivery may be delayed due to circumstances including:

  • Weather conditions
  • Courier or transportation delays
  • High-demand or peak periods
  • Incorrect or incomplete delivery information
  • Serviceability issues
  • Unforeseen circumstances beyond our reasonable control

We will make reasonable efforts to assist customers in case of significant delivery delays.

8. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If an order appears damaged at the time of delivery, please contact E-Commerce Demo as soon as possible with relevant order details and, where possible, photographs or other supporting information.

If a shipment is reported as lost or undelivered, we will coordinate with the relevant shipping partner and take appropriate steps to resolve the issue.

9. Address Changes

Please ensure that your delivery address and contact information are accurate before placing an order.

If you need to request an address change after placing an order, contact us as soon as possible. Address changes may not be possible once an order has been processed or dispatched.
